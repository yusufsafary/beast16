Good questions

FAQ

What is Beast16?

A collection of 4,444 baby beast NFTs on BNB Smart Chain, drawn from 16 species by on-chain seed. Cute faces, tiny fangs, real rarity: one species appears in only about 3% of mints.

How do I mint?

Two lanes open at the same time. Binance Agentic Wallet users mint through their agent with the skill file; the first beast is free for Agent ID holders. Everyone else mints paid on this site at 0.000474 BNB per beast, up to 20 per wallet.

When is the mint?

Mint opens Nov 8 at 12:00 UTC, both lanes at the same moment. Follow @Jake_beast16 on X and keep this page close. Reveal happens 24 hours after mint opens.

What is $BEAST16?

The pack token, supply 10 billion. Each Beast16 NFT can claim 250,000 $BEAST16 once. The launch time is deliberately hidden; there is no countdown and no leaked date.

Can I design my own beast?

Yes, for fun: open Build a Beast and mix species, fur, eyes, mouths and accessories. Minted beasts are decided by the chain, but the mixer shows what the pack can look like.
